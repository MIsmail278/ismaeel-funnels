AI watchlist

Four videos and one 1950 paper. 7 hours 42 minutes of watching. Start here if you want to understand AI.

Before you start

Three of the videos and the paper are free. iHuman is a film you may need to rent or stream.

The list

  1. The Thinking Game

    Film, 1h 24m. Five years inside DeepMind with Demis Hassabis. You see the setbacks, then the moment AlphaFold works. Watch on YouTube

  2. iHuman

    Film, 1h 39m. A 2019 film on AI, power and surveillance. It asks who gains control when AI is deployed. See where to watch it

  3. The A.I. Dilemma

    Talk, 1h 07m. Tristan Harris and Aza Raskin argue that social media was our first contact with AI. Recorded before GPT-4. Watch on YouTube

  4. Deep Dive into LLMs like ChatGPT

    Lecture, 3h 31m. Andrej Karpathy walks through how these models are trained and why they hallucinate. No coding needed. Watch on YouTube

  5. Computing Machinery and Intelligence

    Paper, 1950. Alan Turing asks "Can machines think?", then swaps it for a test: the imitation game. Read the PDF
